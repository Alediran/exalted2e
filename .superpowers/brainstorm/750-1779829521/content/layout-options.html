A — Horizontal Tier Columns

Charms are placed in columns by prerequisite depth. Arrows connect prerequisites. Simpler layout algorithm; always readable regardless of how many charms are in the tree.

B — Free-form DAG (closest to reference image)

Charms positioned freely with lines connecting prerequisites, like the reference image. Visually expressive but harder to lay out algorithmically — complex trees may need manual position hints or can overlap/crowd.

C — Sortable List (no spatial layout)

Flat table/list sorted by prerequisite depth, with charm boxes and status icons. No lines or spatial positioning. Simplest to implement and works for arbitrarily large charm sets, but loses the visual "tree" feel.
