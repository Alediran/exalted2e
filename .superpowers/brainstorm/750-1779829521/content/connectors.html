Right-angle elbows — line goes straight down from source, turns 90°, travels horizontally to align with target column, then drops straight down.

Cross-tier skips travel outside the column boundary (dashed orange) to avoid overlapping charm boxes.

Most engineering-diagram-like; cleanest when many charms share a single prerequisite.

Elbow lines never curve — only horizontal + vertical segments. Very predictable routing.

A is closest to the reference image. B is more organic. C is most structured. Which feels right for a charm tree?
